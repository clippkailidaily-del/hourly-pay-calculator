$21 an Hour Is How Much a Year?

The quick answer, the full breakdown, and what it really means for your paycheck.

$21 an hour = per year (40 hrs/week, 52 weeks)$43,680

$21 an hour is $43,680 a year if you work full time, 40 hours a week for 52 weeks, before taxes. That is the standard conversion employers and payroll departments use: $21 × 2,080 working hours = $43,680. Use it as the benchmark whenever you compare an hourly offer with a salaried one.

What if you take unpaid time off?

The $43,680 headline assumes all 52 weeks are paid. Hourly workers without paid vacation earn less: at 50 paid weeks (two weeks off unpaid), $21 an hour comes to $42,000 a year; at 48 weeks it is $40,320. Salaried jobs at the same headline figure usually keep paying during holidays and vacation, which is one reason a salary of $43,680 is often worth a little more than $21 an hour with no paid time off.

$21 an hour after taxes: how much do you actually take home?

The figures above are gross pay. As a single filer in 2026, a full-time worker at $21 an hour would keep roughly $37,277 a year, about $3,106.41 a month after federal income tax and Social Security/Medicare (FICA). Here is the estimate, line by line:

That works out to about $716.86 a week in your pocket, or an effective $17.92 an hour after tax. Estimate only: single filer, 2026 federal tax brackets with the $16,100 standard deduction, FICA at 7.65%. State income tax is not included (it ranges from 0% in states like Texas and Florida to over 10% in high-tax states), and 401(k) contributions, health insurance premiums or credits would change the result. Your real paychecks will vary, treat this as a planning figure, not tax advice.

Jobs that commonly pay around $21 an hour

Jobs paying around $21 an hour include a lot of office, pharmacy and support work. The BLS lists national medians of $21.53 an hour for customer service representatives and $21.64 for office clerks, with pharmacy technicians at $22.00 and social and human service assistants at $22.08 (May 2025). A $21 offer is therefore right in the middle of the pack for this group of occupations.

Is $21 an hour a good wage?

It depends on where you live and what the job includes, but for scale: the US federal minimum wage is $7.25 an hour, so $21 an hour is about 2.9× the federal minimum. A full-time gross of $43,680 works out to $3,640 a month before tax. Whether that feels comfortable depends mostly on housing costs in your area and whether the job adds benefits, health insurance and a 401(k) match can add 20–40% on top of the wage. Compare any offer on this page's hourly to salary calculator so both sides are in the same units. For comparison, the BLS median for full-time workers was $1,232 a week ($64,064 a year) in the third quarter of 2026, so $43,680 is about 31.8% below that.

Gross, not take-home: all figures here are before federal and state income tax, Social Security and Medicare. Your actual paychecks will be lower.

Frequently asked questions

How much is $21 an hour per year?

$43,680 a year at 40 hours a week for 52 weeks, before taxes ($21 × 2,080 hours).

How much is $21 an hour per month?

About $3,640 a month, before taxes.

What is $21 an hour biweekly?

$1,680 every two weeks, before taxes, 26 paychecks a year.

How much is $21 an hour with overtime?

Hours over 40 in a week are usually paid at 1.5×, $31.50 an hour. Five overtime hours a week would add about $8,190 a year on top of the base $43,680.

How much is $21 an hour after taxes?

About $3,106.41 a month take-home, roughly $37,277 a year, for a single filer after federal income tax and Social Security/Medicare, before any state tax. See the after-tax breakdown above for the full estimate.
