$22 an Hour Is How Much a Year?

The quick answer, the full breakdown, and what it really means for your paycheck.

$22 an hour = per year (40 hrs/week, 52 weeks)$45,760

$22 an hour is $45,760 a year if you work full time, 40 hours a week for 52 weeks, before taxes. That is the standard conversion employers and payroll departments use: $22 × 2,080 working hours = $45,760. Use it as the benchmark whenever you compare an hourly offer with a salaried one.

What if you take unpaid time off?

The $45,760 headline assumes all 52 weeks are paid. Hourly workers without paid vacation earn less: at 50 paid weeks (two weeks off unpaid), $22 an hour comes to $44,000 a year; at 48 weeks it is $42,240. Salaried jobs at the same headline figure usually keep paying during holidays and vacation, which is one reason a salary of $45,760 is often worth a little more than $22 an hour with no paid time off.

$22 an hour after taxes: how much do you actually take home?

The figures above are gross pay. As a single filer in 2026, a full-time worker at $22 an hour would keep roughly $38,948 a year, about $3,245.68 a month after federal income tax and Social Security/Medicare (FICA). Here is the estimate, line by line:

Estimate only: single filer, 2026 federal tax brackets with the $16,100 standard deduction, FICA at 7.65%. State income tax is not included (it ranges from 0% in states like Texas and Florida to over 10% in high-tax states), and 401(k) contributions, health insurance premiums or credits would change the result. Your real paychecks will vary, treat this as a planning figure, not tax advice.

Jobs that commonly pay around $22 an hour

You will see $22 an hour most often in social services, construction and dental or bookkeeping offices. Social and human service assistants have a national median of $22.08 an hour and construction laborers $22.66, while dental assistants come in at $23.11 and bookkeeping, accounting and auditing clerks at $24.36, based on BLS data from May 2025.

Is $22 an hour a good wage?

It depends on where you live and what the job includes, but for scale: the US federal minimum wage is $7.25 an hour, so $22 an hour is about 3.0× the federal minimum. A full-time gross of $45,760 works out to $3,813.33 a month before tax. Whether that feels comfortable depends mostly on housing costs in your area and whether the job adds benefits, health insurance and a 401(k) match can add 20–40% on top of the wage. Compare any offer on this page's hourly to salary calculator so both sides are in the same units. For comparison, the BLS median for full-time workers was $1,232 a week ($64,064 a year) in the third quarter of 2026, so $45,760 is about 28.6% below that.

Gross, not take-home: all figures here are before federal and state income tax, Social Security and Medicare. Your actual paychecks will be lower.

Frequently asked questions

How much is $22 an hour per year?

$45,760 a year at 40 hours a week for 52 weeks, before taxes ($22 × 2,080 hours).

How much is $22 an hour per month?

About $3,813.33 a month, before taxes.

What is $22 an hour biweekly?

$1,760 every two weeks, before taxes, 26 paychecks a year.

How much is $22 an hour with overtime?

Hours over 40 in a week are usually paid at 1.5×, $33.00 an hour. Five overtime hours a week would add about $8,580 a year on top of the base $45,760.

How much is $22 an hour after taxes?

About $3,245.68 a month take-home, roughly $38,948 a year, for a single filer after federal income tax and Social Security/Medicare, before any state tax. See the after-tax breakdown above for the full estimate.
