$40,000 a Year Is How Much an Hour?

The quick answer, the full breakdown, and how it compares with hourly jobs.

$40,000 a year = per hour (40 hrs/week, 52 weeks)$19.23

$40,000 a year is about $19.23 an hour if you work full time, 40 hours a week for 52 weeks, before taxes. The math is the standard conversion in reverse: $40,000 ÷ 2,080 working hours = $19.23 an hour. This is the number to use when you compare a salaried offer with an hourly-paid job.

$40,000 a year after taxes: how much do you actually take home?

What kind of jobs pay around $40,000 a year?

Jobs paying around $40,000 a year are concentrated in care and service work. The median across healthcare support occupations was $18.43 an hour in May 2025, about $38,330 a year, and personal care and service occupations sat at $17.51 an hour, roughly $36,420 a year, according to the Bureau of Labor Statistics.

The single biggest job in this range is home health and personal care aide work: 4.3 million jobs at a $17.21 hourly median, about $35,800 a year.

Is $40,000 a year a good salary?

For scale: at a standard schedule it equals $19.23 an hour, which is about 2.7× the US federal minimum wage of $7.25 an hour, and it works out to $3,333.33 a month before tax. Whether it is good for you depends mainly on your area's housing costs and the benefits package, paid holidays, health insurance and a 401(k) match can add 20–40% on top of a salary. Against the national middle, $40,000 is 37.6% below the BLS median full-time earnings of $64,064 a year ($1,232 a week in the third quarter of 2026).

Gross, not take-home: all figures here are before federal and state income tax, Social Security and Medicare. Your actual paychecks will be lower.

How do I convert any salary to hourly?

Divide the annual salary by the hours you work in a year. For a standard full-time schedule that is salary ÷ 2,080. The calculator does it for any salary, hours and weeks.
