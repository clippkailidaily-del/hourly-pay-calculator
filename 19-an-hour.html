$19 an Hour Is How Much a Year?

The quick answer, the full breakdown, and what it really means for your paycheck.

$19 an hour = per year (40 hrs/week, 52 weeks)$39,520

$19 an hour is $39,520 a year if you work full time, 40 hours a week for 52 weeks, before taxes. That is the standard conversion employers and payroll departments use: $19 × 2,080 working hours = $39,520. Use it as the benchmark whenever you compare an hourly offer with a salaried one.

What if you take unpaid time off?

The $39,520 headline assumes all 52 weeks are paid. Hourly workers without paid vacation earn less: at 50 paid weeks (two weeks off unpaid), $19 an hour comes to $38,000 a year; at 48 weeks it is $36,480. Salaried jobs at the same headline figure usually keep paying during holidays and vacation, which is one reason a salary of $39,520 is often worth a little more than $19 an hour with no paid time off.

$19 an hour after taxes: how much do you actually take home?

The figures above are gross pay. As a single filer in 2026, a full-time worker at $19 an hour would keep roughly $33,934 a year, about $2,827.86 a month after federal income tax and Social Security/Medicare (FICA). Here is the estimate, line by line:

Estimate only: single filer, 2026 federal tax brackets with the $16,100 standard deduction, FICA at 7.65%. State income tax is not included (it ranges from 0% in states like Texas and Florida to over 10% in high-tax states), and 401(k) contributions, health insurance premiums or credits would change the result. Your real paychecks will vary, treat this as a planning figure, not tax advice.

Jobs that commonly pay around $19 an hour

At about $19 an hour, the typical jobs lean physical or public-facing. Laborers and freight, stock and material movers have a national median of $19.35 an hour and counter and rental clerks $19.86, while nursing assistants ($20.32) and tellers ($20.69) sit just over the line (BLS, May 2025). Offers at exactly $19 are common in the first two groups and as starting pay in the second two.

Is $19 an hour a good wage?

It depends on where you live and what the job includes, but for scale: the US federal minimum wage is $7.25 an hour, so $19 an hour is about 2.6× the federal minimum. A full-time gross of $39,520 works out to $3,293.33 a month before tax. Whether that feels comfortable depends mostly on housing costs in your area and whether the job adds benefits, health insurance and a 401(k) match can add 20–40% on top of the wage. Compare any offer on this page's hourly to salary calculator so both sides are in the same units. For comparison, the BLS median for full-time workers was $1,232 a week ($64,064 a year) in the third quarter of 2026, so $39,520 is about 38.3% below that.

Gross, not take-home: all figures here are before federal and state income tax, Social Security and Medicare. Your actual paychecks will be lower.

Frequently asked questions

How much is $19 an hour per year?

What is $19 an hour biweekly?

How much is $19 an hour with overtime?

Hours over 40 in a week are usually paid at 1.5×, $28.50 an hour. Five overtime hours a week would add about $7,410 a year on top of the base $39,520.

How much is $19 an hour after taxes?

About $2,827.86 a month take-home, roughly $33,934 a year, for a single filer after federal income tax and Social Security/Medicare, before any state tax. See the after-tax breakdown above for the full estimate.
