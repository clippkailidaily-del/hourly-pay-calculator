$25 an Hour Is How Much a Year?

The quick answer, the full breakdown, and what it really means for your paycheck.

$25 an hour = per year (40 hrs/week, 52 weeks)$52,000

$25 an hour is $52,000 a year if you work full time, 40 hours a week for 52 weeks, before taxes. That is the standard conversion employers and payroll departments use: $25 × 2,080 working hours = $52,000. Use it as the benchmark whenever you compare an hourly offer with a salaried one.

What if you take unpaid time off?

The $52,000 headline assumes all 52 weeks are paid. Hourly workers without paid vacation earn less: at 50 paid weeks (two weeks off unpaid), $25 an hour comes to $50,000 a year; at 48 weeks it is $48,000. Salaried jobs at the same headline figure usually keep paying during holidays and vacation, which is one reason a salary of $52,000 is often worth a little more than $25 an hour with no paid time off.

$25 an hour after taxes: how much do you actually take home?

The figures above are gross pay. As a single filer in 2026, a full-time worker at $25 an hour would keep roughly $43,962 a year, about $3,663.50 a month after federal income tax and Social Security/Medicare (FICA). Here is the estimate, line by line:

That works out to about $845.42 a week in your pocket, or an effective $21.14 an hour after tax. Estimate only: single filer, 2026 federal tax brackets with the $16,100 standard deduction, FICA at 7.65%. State income tax is not included (it ranges from 0% in states like Texas and Florida to over 10% in high-tax states), and 401(k) contributions, health insurance premiums or credits would change the result. Your real paychecks will vary, treat this as a planning figure, not tax advice.

Jobs that commonly pay around $25 an hour

Look at who actually earns about $25 an hour and it is mostly skilled trades and public-safety work. The Bureau of Labor Statistics put the median for welders, cutters, solderers and brazers at $25.84 an hour in May 2025, with roofers close behind at $26.65.

Correctional officers and jailers earned a median of $28.34, and firefighters $28.50, jobs where experience, certification or risk push pay above the middle of the hourly range.

Is $25 an hour a good wage?

It depends on where you live and what the job includes, but for scale: the US federal minimum wage is $7.25 an hour, so $25 an hour is about 3.4× the federal minimum. A full-time gross of $52,000 works out to $4,333.33 a month before tax. Whether that feels comfortable depends mostly on housing costs in your area and whether the job adds benefits, health insurance and a 401(k) match can add 20–40% on top of the wage. Compare any offer on this page's hourly to salary calculator so both sides are in the same units. Measured against the whole country, $52,000 a year is 18.8% below the BLS median for full-time workers, $1,232 a week in the third quarter of 2026, or about $64,064 a year.

Gross, not take-home: all figures here are before federal and state income tax, Social Security and Medicare. Your actual paychecks will be lower.

Frequently asked questions

How much is $25 an hour per year?

$52,000 a year at 40 hours a week for 52 weeks, before taxes ($25 × 2,080 hours).

How much is $25 an hour per month?

About $4,333.33 a month, before taxes.

What is $25 an hour biweekly?

$2,000 every two weeks, before taxes, 26 paychecks a year.

How much is $25 an hour with overtime?

Hours over 40 in a week are usually paid at 1.5×, $37.50 an hour. Five overtime hours a week would add about $9,750 a year on top of the base $52,000.

How much is $25 an hour after taxes?

About $3,663.50 a month take-home, roughly $43,962 a year, for a single filer after federal income tax and Social Security/Medicare, before any state tax. See the after-tax breakdown above for the full estimate.
