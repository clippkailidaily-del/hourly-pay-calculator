How to convert hourly pay to salary

The formula is simple: Annual salary = hourly wage × hours per week × weeks per year. A standard full-time job is 40 hours a week for 52 weeks, which is 2,080 working hours a year. That gives you a shortcut used by payroll departments everywhere: hourly wage × 2,080 = annual salary, and every $1 an hour is worth $2,080 a year.

Worked example: $25 an hour × 40 hours × 52 weeks = $52,000 a year. Monthly that is $52,000 ÷ 12 = $4,333, and a biweekly paycheck is $52,000 ÷ 26 = $2,000.

Adjust the weeks if you take unpaid time off

The 2,080-hour shortcut assumes every week is paid, including holidays and vacation. If you are a contractor or your job does not pay for time off, set the weeks per year lower — 50 weeks for two unpaid weeks off, for example. At $25 an hour and 50 weeks, the real figure is $50,000, not $52,000.

What about overtime?

Under the US Fair Labor Standards Act, most hourly employees are paid 1.5× their rate for hours over 40 in a week. If you regularly work 45 hours a week at $22 an hour, those 5 extra hours pay $33 each — an extra $165 a week, or $8,580 a year, on top of the $45,760 base. To model that here, raise the hours per week and remember the extra hours are worth more than the base rate.

Hourly vs salary — which offer is better?

Comparing a job offer is easier once both are on the same basis. A $60,000 salary equals $28.85 an hour at 40 hours a week — but only $19.23 an hour if the job really takes 60 hours a week. Salaried roles often include paid holidays, vacation and benefits such as health insurance and a 401(k) match, which can add 20–40% to total compensation. Convert first, then compare the whole package, not just the headline number.

Please note: every figure on this page is gross pay before taxes. Your take-home pay will be lower after federal income tax, state tax, Social Security and Medicare (FICA), and any benefit deductions. This calculator is for planning and comparison, not tax advice.

Frequently asked questions

How do you calculate annual salary from hourly pay?

Multiply your hourly wage by your hours per week, then by your weeks per year. Full-time shortcut: wage × 2,080. Example: $20 × 2,080 = $41,600 a year.

What is $25 an hour annually?

$52,000 a year before taxes, at 40 hours a week for 52 weeks — $4,333 a month and $2,000 every two weeks.

What is $20 an hour annually?

$41,600 a year before taxes — $3,467 a month and $1,600 every two weeks.

What is $15 an hour annually?

$31,200 a year before taxes — $2,600 a month and $1,200 every two weeks.

How many working hours are in a year?

2,080 for a standard full-time schedule (40 × 52). Part-time at 30 hours a week is 1,560 hours; 37.5 hours a week is 1,950 hours.

How do I convert a salary back to hourly?

Divide the annual salary by 2,080 (for a 40-hour week). A $75,000 salary is $75,000 ÷ 2,080 ≈ $36.06 an hour.

Is this calculator before or after tax?

Before tax. All amounts are gross. Take-home pay depends on your tax filing status, state, and deductions.

Does this include overtime pay?

The calculator prices every hour at the base rate. For overtime, add your extra hours at 1.5× separately, as explained in the overtime section above.
