$100,000 a Year Is How Much an Hour?

The quick answer, the full breakdown, and how it compares with hourly jobs.

$100,000 a year = per hour (40 hrs/week, 52 weeks)$48.08

$100,000 a year is about $48.08 an hour if you work full time, 40 hours a week for 52 weeks, before taxes. The math is the standard conversion in reverse: $100,000 ÷ 2,080 working hours = $48.08 an hour. This is the number to use when you compare a salaried offer with an hourly-paid job.

$100,000 a year after taxes: how much do you actually take home?

The figures above are gross pay. As a single filer in 2026, a worker earning $100,000 a year would keep roughly $79,180 a year, about $6,598.33 a month after federal income tax and Social Security/Medicare (FICA). Here is the estimate, line by line:

Estimate only: single filer, 2026 federal tax brackets with the $16,100 standard deduction, FICA at 7.65%. State income tax is not included (it ranges from 0% in states like Texas and Florida to over 10% in high-tax states), and 401(k) contributions, health insurance premiums or credits would change the result. Your real paychecks will vary, treat this as a planning figure, not tax advice.

What kind of jobs pay around $100,000 a year?

Six-figure medians belong mostly to technical professions. Architecture and engineering occupations had a median of $47.85 an hour in May 2025, $99,520 a year, and computer and mathematical occupations $52.54 an hour, or $109,280 a year, according to the Bureau of Labor Statistics.

The largest single high-wage occupation is registered nursing: 3.38 million jobs at a $46.90 hourly median, about $97,550 a year, with general and operations managers just above at $50.85 an hour, roughly $105,770 a year.

Is $100,000 a year a good salary?

For scale: at a standard schedule it equals $48.08 an hour, which is about 6.6× the US federal minimum wage of $7.25 an hour, and it works out to $8,333.33 a month before tax. Whether it is good for you depends mainly on your area's housing costs and the benefits package, paid holidays, health insurance and a 401(k) match can add 20–40% on top of a salary. How do I convert any salary to hourly?

Divide the annual salary by the hours you work in a year. For a standard full-time schedule that is salary ÷ 2,080. The calculator does it for any salary, hours and weeks.

About $6,598.33 a month take-home, roughly $79,180 a year, for a single filer after federal income tax and Social Security/Medicare, before any state tax. See the after-tax breakdown above for the full estimate.
