$50,000 a Year Is How Much an Hour?

The quick answer, the full breakdown, and how it compares with hourly jobs.

$50,000 a year = per hour (40 hrs/week, 52 weeks)$24.04

$50,000 a year is about $24.04 an hour if you work full time, 40 hours a week for 52 weeks, before taxes. The math is the standard conversion in reverse: $50,000 ÷ 2,080 working hours = $24.04 an hour. This is the number to use when you compare a salaried offer with an hourly-paid job.

$50,000 a year after taxes: how much do you actually take home?

The figures above are gross pay. As a single filer in 2026, a worker earning $50,000 a year would keep roughly $42,355 a year, about $3,529.58 a month after federal income tax and Social Security/Medicare (FICA). Here is the estimate, line by line:

State income tax is not included (it ranges from 0% in states like Texas and Florida to over 10% in high-tax states), and 401(k) contributions, health insurance premiums or credits would change the result. Your real paychecks will vary, treat this as a planning figure, not tax advice.

What kind of jobs pay around $50,000 a year?

Two very large groups of jobs centre on this salary. Protective service occupations had a median of $24.08 an hour in May 2025, about $50,090 a year, and office and administrative support occupations a median of $22.81 an hour, roughly $47,450 a year, per the Bureau of Labor Statistics.

$50,000 is also close to the middle of the entire US pay distribution: the median annual wage across all occupations was $50,980 in May 2025.

Is $50,000 a year a good salary?

For scale: at a standard schedule it equals $24.04 an hour, which is about 3.3× the US federal minimum wage of $7.25 an hour, and it works out to $4,166.67 a month before tax. Whether it is good for you depends mainly on your area's housing costs and the benefits package, paid holidays, health insurance and a 401(k) match can add 20–40% on top of a salary. Compared with full-time workers overall, $50,000 is 22.0% below the BLS median of $64,064 a year ($1,232 a week in the third quarter of 2026).

Gross, not take-home: all figures here are before federal and state income tax, Social Security and Medicare. Your actual paychecks will be lower.

How do I convert any salary to hourly?

Divide the annual salary by the hours you work in a year. For a standard full-time schedule that is salary ÷ 2,080. The calculator does it for any salary, hours and weeks.

How much is $50,000 a year after taxes?

About $3,529.58 a month take-home, roughly $42,355 a year, for a single filer after federal income tax and Social Security/Medicare, before any state tax. See the after-tax breakdown above for the full estimate.
