$50,000 a Year Is How Much an Hour?

The quick answer, the full breakdown, and how it compares with hourly jobs.

$50,000 a year = per hour (40 hrs/week, 52 weeks)$24.04

$50,000 a year is about $24.04 an hour if you work full time — 40 hours a week for 52 weeks — before taxes. The math is the standard conversion in reverse: $50,000 ÷ 2,080 working hours = $24.04 an hour. This is the number to use when you compare a salaried offer with an hourly-paid job.

Is $50,000 a year a good salary?

For scale: at a standard schedule it equals $24.04 an hour, which is about 3.3× the US federal minimum wage of $7.25 an hour, and it works out to $4,166.67 a month before tax. Whether it is good for you depends mainly on your area's housing costs and the benefits package — paid holidays, health insurance and a 401(k) match can add 20–40% on top of a salary. How do I convert any salary to hourly?

Divide the annual salary by the hours you work in a year. For a standard full-time schedule that is salary ÷ 2,080. The calculator does it for any salary, hours and weeks.
