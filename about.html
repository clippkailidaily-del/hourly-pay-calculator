About this calculator

Hourly Pay Calculator is a free, one-page tool built for workers in the United States who get job offers, raises or schedules quoted in different units, dollars an hour, a week, a month or a year, and need to compare them on equal terms.

Written by Kaili Bill, creator of the Hourly Pay Calculator — a free tool built to help US workers compare hourly wages and salaries on equal terms.

Enter an hourly wage and your schedule, and the calculator instantly shows the equivalent annual salary, monthly pay, biweekly paycheck and weekly pay, using the same math payroll departments use: a standard full-time year of 2,080 hours (40 hours × 52 weeks).

The page also includes a full conversion table for wages from $10 to $100 an hour, worked examples, and plain-English explanations of overtime, unpaid time off, and the difference between gross and take-home pay.

How the numbers are calculated

Annual pay = hourly wage × hours per week × weeks per year. Monthly = annual ÷ 12. Biweekly = annual ÷ 26. Weekly = hourly wage × hours per week. All results are gross pay, before taxes and deductions, and are estimates for planning and comparison, not financial or tax advice.
