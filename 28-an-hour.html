$28 an Hour Is How Much a Year?

The quick answer, the full breakdown, and what it really means for your paycheck.

$28 an hour = per year (40 hrs/week, 52 weeks)$58,240

$28 an hour is $58,240 a year if you work full time, 40 hours a week for 52 weeks, before taxes. That is the standard conversion employers and payroll departments use: $28 × 2,080 working hours = $58,240. Use it as the benchmark whenever you compare an hourly offer with a salaried one.

What if you take unpaid time off?

The $58,240 headline assumes all 52 weeks are paid. Hourly workers without paid vacation earn less: at 50 paid weeks (two weeks off unpaid), $28 an hour comes to $56,000 a year; at 48 weeks it is $53,760. Salaried jobs at the same headline figure usually keep paying during holidays and vacation, which is one reason a salary of $58,240 is often worth a little more than $28 an hour with no paid time off.

$28 an hour after taxes: how much do you actually take home?

The figures above are gross pay. As a single filer in 2026, a full-time worker at $28 an hour would keep roughly $48,976 a year, about $4,081.32 a month after federal income tax and Social Security/Medicare (FICA). Here is the estimate, line by line:

Estimate only: single filer, 2026 federal tax brackets with the $16,100 standard deduction, FICA at 7.65%. State income tax is not included (it ranges from 0% in states like Texas and Florida to over 10% in high-tax states), and 401(k) contributions, health insurance premiums or credits would change the result. Your real paychecks will vary, treat this as a planning figure, not tax advice.

Jobs that commonly pay around $28 an hour

This is solidly skilled-trades territory. Firefighters had a median wage of $28.50 an hour in May 2025 in BLS data, carpenters $29.12, and heating, air conditioning and refrigeration mechanics and installers $29.33. Electricians, at a $30.38 median, sit just above the band.

Jobs paying around $28 an hour usually ask for an apprenticeship, a licence or a few years on the tools, it is experienced-worker pay, not a starting rate.

Is $28 an hour a good wage?

It depends on where you live and what the job includes, but for scale: the US federal minimum wage is $7.25 an hour, so $28 an hour is about 3.9× the federal minimum. A full-time gross of $58,240 works out to $4,853.33 a month before tax. Whether that feels comfortable depends mostly on housing costs in your area and whether the job adds benefits, health insurance and a 401(k) match can add 20–40% on top of the wage. Compare any offer on this page's hourly to salary calculator so both sides are in the same units. Nationally, $58,240 a year is 9.1% below the median full-time earnings of $64,064 a year reported by the BLS for the third quarter of 2026 ($1,232 a week).

Gross, not take-home: all figures here are before federal and state income tax, Social Security and Medicare. Your actual paychecks will be lower.

Frequently asked questions

How much is $28 an hour per year?

$58,240 a year at 40 hours a week for 52 weeks, before taxes ($28 × 2,080 hours).

How much is $28 an hour per month?

About $4,853.33 a month, before taxes.

What is $28 an hour biweekly?

$2,240 every two weeks, before taxes, 26 paychecks a year.

How much is $28 an hour with overtime?

Hours over 40 in a week are usually paid at 1.5×, $42.00 an hour. Five overtime hours a week would add about $10,920 a year on top of the base $58,240.

How much is $28 an hour after taxes?

About $4,081.32 a month take-home, roughly $48,976 a year, for a single filer after federal income tax and Social Security/Medicare, before any state tax. See the after-tax breakdown above for the full estimate.
